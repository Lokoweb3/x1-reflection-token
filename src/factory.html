What buyers can check on-chain

  • The tax can never be changed (no fee authority).
  • The supply is fixed (mint authority revoked).
  • The whole supply goes into the pool; the creator starts with no tokens.
  • The launch liquidity is locked in an NFT, forever or until the date shown.
  • Only the token's own distributor wallet can collect its tax, and it splits it the same way every time.
  • The creator's 10% vests 7 days in an on-chain vault; only the LP NFT's holder can claim it.
