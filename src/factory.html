Launch progress

    Create the token
    Tax fixed forever, supply fixed, launch fee paid
  2. 2
    Create the XDEX pool
    Your tokens + XNT become the starting liquidity
  3. 3
    Lock the LP in an NFT
    You get the NFT, which collects the pool's trading fees
  4. 4
    Start distributions
    The tax is collected and paid to holders in XNT

What buyers can check on-chain

  • The tax can never be changed (no fee authority).
  • The supply is fixed (mint authority revoked).
  • The launch liquidity is locked in an NFT, forever or until the date shown.
  • Collected tax can only be withdrawn by the token's own distributor wallet.
