Questions

What is a tax (reflection) token?

A token that takes a small percentage of every transfer, including every buy and sell. Here that tax isn't kept by the team. It's sold for XNT and paid to everyone who holds the token, in proportion to how much they hold.

How do holders get paid?

About every 30 minutes, the token's distributor collects the tax, sells it on XDEX and sends XNT directly to holders' wallets. There's nothing to claim. Very small shares build up until they reach 0.001 XNT, then they're sent.

Who gets the tax that goes to liquidity?

Nobody. It's added to the pool, and the LP tokens it creates are burned, so that liquidity stays in the pool for good and the price gets steadier over time.

What does the burn do?

If the creator turns it on, that share of each cycle's collected tax is burned outright, before anything is sold. The token supply goes down every cycle, and because those tokens are never sold, there's less selling pressure too. The creator picks the share at launch (up to 50%), and it can't be changed later.

What does the LP NFT do?

It holds the launch liquidity. Whoever owns the NFT can collect the trading fees that liquidity earns and can sell or transfer that right, but can't withdraw the liquidity itself. With a timed lock, the NFT holder can take the LP back after the date.

Can the creator rug?

They can't change the tax, print tokens or pull locked liquidity. They can still sell tokens they hold, like anyone else, so check how much of the supply went into the pool and whether the lock is forever.

Is it audited?

Not yet. The code is open source and has been tested on X1 testnet and against attack cases, but the lock program hasn't had an independent audit. Only use what you can afford to lose.
