Questions at the register

What is a tax (reflection) token?

A token that takes a small percentage of every transfer, including every buy and sell. Here that tax isn't kept by the team. It's sold for XNT and paid to everyone who holds the token, in proportion to how much they hold.

How do holders get paid?

About every 30 minutes, the token's distributor collects the tax, sells it on XDEX and sends XNT directly to holders' wallets. There's nothing to claim. Very small shares build up until they reach 0.001 XNT, then they're sent.

What's "Distribute now"?

If you don't want to wait for the next 30-minute run, anyone can press Distribute now on the launch page and pay a small tip (0.005 XNT) that covers the gas. The token's full distribution runs right away, and the person who pressed it earns 1% of that run's holder payout, up to 0.05 XNT. It only works when enough tax is waiting, at most once every 10 minutes per token.

Who gets the tax that goes to liquidity?

Nobody. It's added to the pool, and the LP tokens it creates are burned, so that liquidity stays in the pool for good and the price gets steadier over time.

What does the burn do?

If the creator turns it on, that share of each cycle's collected tax is burned outright, before anything is sold. The token supply goes down every cycle, and because those tokens are never sold, there's less selling pressure too. The creator picks the share at launch (up to 50%; liquidity plus burn can't pass 55%, because 10% goes to the creator and holders always get at least 35%), and it can't be changed later.

How do creators earn?

Creators start with no tokens, so instead every token pays its creator 10% of its tax. It's sold and swapped to USDC (XNT on testnet), then held in an on-chain vault tied to the launch's LP-lock NFT. Each deposit vests for 7 days, then whoever holds the NFT can claim it. Sell the NFT and you sell the income with it.

What does the LP NFT do?

It holds the launch liquidity. Whoever owns the NFT collects the trading fees that liquidity earns and the token's creator rewards, and can sell or transfer both, but can't withdraw the liquidity itself. With a timed lock, the NFT holder can take the LP back after the date and keeps the NFT for the rewards.

Can the creator rug?

They can't change the tax, print tokens or pull locked liquidity. They also start with no tokens: every launch puts 100% of the supply into the pool, so there's no dev bag to dump. What's left to check is whether the lock is forever or timed.

Is it audited?

Not yet. The code is open source and has been tested on X1 testnet and against attack cases, but the lock program hasn't had an independent audit. Only use what you can afford to lose.
