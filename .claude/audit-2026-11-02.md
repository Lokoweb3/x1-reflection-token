## Co-signer redesign: stopped, next design to try after the move

Owner's decision, 2 Nov 2026 (night): stop the redesign. `audit-cosigner-scan` (`42265aa`, on `5f8234d`) stays local and isn't patched. The Squads move runs on `main`'s co-signer at `--max-moved-bps 100`, in `--dry-run` first. Finding 23 stays open on the held quorum branch; `main`'s co-signer doesn't have that defect.

**What it was:** the co-signer records its own scan of all token accounts about every 60 s, and for a list at slot S requires each stated balance within the scans either side of S, with the stated sum at most the larger scan's total. No transaction history. Fresh clone: 438/438 TS, tsc clean; agent's rehearsal passed on a local validator with the testnet Squads program.

**Codex review (`x1-reviewer-report-cs.txt`): do not approve, not safer than `main` at `--max-moved-bps 100`. Three blockers, all confirmed by Claude reading the code; blocker 2 also assumes standard Token-2022 SetAuthority on X1:**
1. Jointly impossible balances pass (`src/list-verify.ts:160-171`). Scan A: X=100, H=100, Z=100. Attacker moves X→Y and H exits, so scan B: Y=95, Z=100. Stating X=100, Y=95, Z=100 and omitting H passes every range and the sum (295 ≤ 300). Attacker's share goes from 33.3% to 66.1%. Needs another holder's exit inside one scan gap within the age limit.
2. Cheap whole-list veto (`src/list-verify.ts:160-168`). A holder rotates the owner of one funded token account among fresh wallets. A snapshot landing on a wallet neither scan saw fails the list. Costs one minHolding position plus fees, no taxed trades.
3. A timed-out vote keeps running (`src/cosigner-loop.ts:61-65`). The timeout releases the lock but doesn't stop the work, so it can broadcast late. Code after the last slot check can also send a list past the age limit.

Should-fix: scans not tied to the canonical fork; permissions below the scan directory unchecked; no alert when the RPC's slot stops advancing. Off-chain approve + execute narrows but doesn't close the approval-to-execution gap: the real fix is an expiry on `publish_list_checked`, blocked until the upgrade authority moves.

Unresolved: honest rejection rate. Agent's replay 11/392 (2.8%) on lists 749-755; Codex's fixture replay 0/990 on 750-755. Not reconciled.

**Next design to try, after the move: one 1% ambiguity budget.** Total movement between the two scans plus any stated balance outside them must stay within 1% of weight, otherwise the list is rebuilt.
- Expected effect (reasoned, not built or reviewed): overstatement capped at 1% of weight, tighter than `main`'s larger of 5% of own stated balance and 1% of weight. Sales after the snapshot still pass. A veto needs about 1% of weight, as on `main` today.
- Cost: up to 1% of padding even in quiet periods. A smaller budget for out-of-range balances narrows that but makes vetoing cheaper.
- Blocker 3 and the should-fixes need fixing whichever rule is used.

**X1 evidence kept for it (read-only, 2 Nov 2026, `x1-test/` in the scratchpad):** one scan a minute for two hours on the public RPC. See the totals line below. No new list landed and no balance changed, so scans were never compared against a real list during live trading.
